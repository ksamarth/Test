BRIGHTSMILE DENTAL PARTNERS, LLC
ASSOCIATE DENTIST EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Employment Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between BrightSmile Dental
Partners, LLC, a Texas limited liability company ("Employer"), and Dr. Jordan Rivera,
D.D.S. ("Provider").

1. POSITION AND DUTIES
Provider is employed as an Associate Dentist practicing general dentistry at Employer's
Frisco, Texas office and such other Employer locations as Employer may reasonably
designate. Provider shall work five (5) days per week on a schedule set by Employer.

2. TERM
The initial term of this Agreement is two (2) years, automatically renewing for
successive one (1) year terms unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal
at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term.

3. COMPENSATION
3.1 Provider shall be paid the greater of (a) a daily guarantee of Six Hundred Fifty
Dollars ($650) per day worked during the first ninety (90) days of employment, or
(b) thirty percent (30%) of Provider's monthly Net Collections.
3.2 After the first ninety (90) days, Provider shall be compensated solely under
Section 3.1(b).
3.3 "Net Collections" means amounts actually collected by Employer for dental services
personally performed by Provider, less laboratory fees, implant and specialty supply
costs attributable to Provider's procedures, and refunds or chargebacks.
3.4 Provider shall receive a signing bonus of Fifteen Thousand Dollars ($15,000),
payable in the first regular payroll. If Provider's employment terminates for any
reason within twenty-four (24) months of the Start Date, Provider shall repay the
signing bonus in full within thirty (30) days of termination.

4. BENEFITS
Provider is eligible to participate in Employer's group health plan and 401(k) plan
per plan terms. Provider shall receive five (5) days of paid time off per contract
year and Five Hundred Dollars ($500) annually toward continuing education.

5. MALPRACTICE INSURANCE
Employer shall maintain professional liability insurance covering Provider on a
claims-made basis during the term of employment. Upon termination of employment for
any reason, Provider shall, at Provider's sole expense, obtain extended reporting
period ("tail") coverage and provide proof thereof to Employer.

6. TERMINATION
6.1 Employer may terminate this Agreement without cause upon thirty (30) days written
notice. Provider may terminate this Agreement without cause upon ninety (90) days
written notice.
6.2 Employer may terminate this Agreement immediately for cause, which includes,
without limitation, Provider's failure to meet Employer's production standards,
conduct that in Employer's sole judgment is detrimental to Employer's reputation, or
violation of any Employer policy.

7. RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS
7.1 During employment and for twenty-four (24) months following termination for any
reason, Provider shall not practice dentistry within fifteen (15) miles of any office
operated by Employer or its affiliates at which Provider provided services on one or
more occasions.
7.2 For twenty-four (24) months following termination, Provider shall not solicit any
patient of Employer or induce any employee of Employer to leave Employer.
7.3 Provider acknowledges that a breach of this Section would cause irreparable harm
and agrees that Employer shall be entitled to liquidated damages of One Hundred
Thousand Dollars ($100,000) in addition to injunctive relief.

8. PATIENT RECORDS
All patient records, charts, and goodwill are and shall remain the sole property of
Employer.

9. INDEPENDENT JUDGMENT
Nothing herein shall interfere with Provider's exercise of independent clinical
judgment; provided, however, that Provider shall comply with Employer's treatment
planning protocols and scheduling standards.

10. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Texas.

AGREED:
BrightSmile Dental Partners, LLC          Dr. Jordan Rivera, D.D.S.
