Before you sign, ask Bailey.

The contract review your mentor would give you: a grade for your offer, the money modeled out, the cost of leaving priced, every term benchmarked against market — and the negotiation email already written.

Bailey is an educational tool. It does not provide legal, financial, or tax advice, and it is not a substitute for review by a licensed attorney or CPA. Earnings figures are illustrative estimates built on stated assumptions, not income promises. Have any contract reviewed by a professional before you sign.
